PDF Text Replacement Editor

Click replacement text to edit it and show its shape outline again. Drag replacement text to move it, or drag the blue shape handle to move the shape separately. Click a line to select it, then use Remove line or Delete. Use Undo or Ctrl+Z to restore a change.

Note: The old text is covered with a white box; it is not removed from the PDF data.
